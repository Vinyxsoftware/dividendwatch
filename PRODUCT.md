# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Retail investors in the DACH region (Switzerland, Germany, Austria) — working adults investing part-time, comfortable reading numbers but not finance professionals. Primarily Swiss users who care about Verrechnungssteuer, dividend sustainability, and building passive income over time. They open this tool to answer a specific question: "Is this stock worth adding to my savings plan?" and close it once they have the answer.

## Product Purpose

DividendWatch cuts through the noise of professional finance platforms and gives retail investors a clean, fast answer: which dividend stocks are worth holding, how much passive income they can expect, and whether those dividends are sustainable. It is not a trading platform, not a broker, not investment advice — it is a research and planning tool that respects the user's time and intelligence.

## Brand Personality

Smart, calm, precise. Robinhood's clarity meets a Swiss watchmaker's attention to detail. No hype, no noise, no information overload. The interface is confident without being loud.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS / AI tool aesthetic: no purple-on-white gradients, no floating semi-transparent cards used decoratively, no "hero metric" template (big number, small label, gradient behind it), no identical card grids.
- Old-school finance data walls: no cluttered tables with 20 columns, no Bloomberg-style information density for its own sake.
- Crypto / Web3 hype aesthetics: no aggressive glows, no neon-on-black, no visual excess.

## Design Principles

1. **Data earns its place.** Every data point shown must help the user make a decision. If it doesn't, cut it.
2. **Calm confidence.** The UI doesn't shout. It informs. Emphasis is reserved for what genuinely matters.
3. **Swiss precision.** Numbers are formatted correctly, aligned properly, and never approximate where exactness is possible.
4. **Clarity over decoration.** Glass effects, gradients, and shadows serve comprehension — not mood boards.
5. **Accessible wealth.** Financial tools should feel within reach for ordinary people, not gatekept by jargon or complexity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Financial numbers must meet contrast requirements. Reduced motion respected via `prefers-reduced-motion`. All interactive elements keyboard-navigable with visible focus indicators.
